Privacy Policy

SF Capital Data Warehouse · Effective 20 October 2026

1. Who uses the application

SF Capital Data Warehouse ("the application") is a private, internal tool operated by SF Capital. Only SF Capital's owner uses it, and only with SF Capital's own Google account. It is not offered to the public, and no other person's Google account is connected to it.

2. Google user data the application accesses

With the account holder's consent, the application uses the Google Drive API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive to list, read, create and update files and folders in SF Capital's own Google Drive. These files are SF Capital's research datasets, research archives, model files and operating records. The application does not access Gmail, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service. It reads the signed-in account's email address only to confirm that the correct account is connected.

3. How the data is used

Google Drive data is used only to store, organise, back up and retrieve SF Capital's own research data for SF Capital's internal research. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build user profiles. It is not used to develop, improve or train generalised artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

4. Sharing

Google user data is not shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party. The only exceptions are when the law requires it, or where processing by SF Capital's own hosting infrastructure is needed to run the application.

5. Storage and security

Authorisation credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored only as access-restricted secrets on SF Capital's private servers. They are never published or placed in source code. Research data remains in SF Capital's Google Drive and on SF Capital's private servers. Access is limited to SF Capital.

6. Retention and deletion

Files remain in SF Capital's Google Drive until SF Capital deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored authorisation stops working, and SF Capital deletes it from its servers.

7. Compliance with Google's policy

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be sent to SF Capital through the developer contact shown on the application's Google consent screen.
